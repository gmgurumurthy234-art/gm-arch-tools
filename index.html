About GM ARCH TOOLS

GM

GM ARCH TOOLS

Architecture Workflow Platform

GM ARCH TOOLS is an all-in-one computational architecture platform created by Guru Murthy. It bridges the gap between statutory municipal bye-laws (NBC 2016, Model Bye-Laws, URDPFI) and real-world architectural design workflows.

CORE PRINCIPLES
  • 13 Specialized Modules: From statutory calculations to parametric design, codes, estimation, and AI assistants.
  • 35 Arch Calculatives: Interactive 8-stage calculations for site, FSI, coverage, setbacks, parking, height, and carpet.
  • Deterministic & Zero AI Latency: 100% mathematical local precision with instant live updates.
  • Strict Dimensional Isolation: Units converter enforces separation between linear length and surface area.
